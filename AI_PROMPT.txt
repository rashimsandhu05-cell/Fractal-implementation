1. Fractal selection and approach
   I asked for help choosing a fractal that demonstrates self-similarity and can

   I asked how to express the Sierpiński rule as a recursive function. The advice

   I asked for guidance on drawing a filled triangle from three coordinate pairs.
   The implementation uses Python's turtle module to move between vertices, close
   the shape, and apply a fill colour.

5. Presentation and parameters
   I asked how to make the output easier to inspect. The notebook sets a white
   drawing background, hides the Turtle cursor, uses a colour cycle for recursion
   levels, and stores the recursion depth in a variable so it can be adjusted.

6. Review of the implementation
   I asked for a review of the completed notebook. The review confirmed that the
   three recursive calls represent the top, lower-left, and lower-right triangles,
